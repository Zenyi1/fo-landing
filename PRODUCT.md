# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Pharma originators — commercial and BD leads at companies holding FDA/EMA-approved (or late Phase 3) medicines — arriving from LinkedIn, a deck, or an intro. They are evaluating whether firstocean is real and credible, not reading an argument. Secondary: distributors and investors who land on the same page.

## Product Purpose

firstocean registers and commercializes approved medicines in markets outside the core (Latin America, the Middle East and North Africa, Southeast Asia). It becomes the licence holder, runs the commercial operation, and is paid out of what the medicine earns in the territory. The site's job is now presence, not persuasion: identity + a few quiet lines + a way to get in touch.

## Positioning

"The commercial operation you would otherwise have to build." (confirmed brand line, from the LinkedIn banner.)

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single page only (confirmed 2026-09-17): every other route is deleted — /markets, /originators, /distributors, /privacy, /terms, and the /api/valuation route and its components.
- Contact: a form that does not expose the email address (hugo@first-ocean.com stays out of markup). Delivery mechanism undecided — needs an email/delivery integration.
- Copy budget: a few quiet lines (what, where, how paid) — no sections, no argument structure.
- Stack: Next.js App Router, Tailwind v4, deployed on Vercel. PostHog + Vercel Analytics stay.

## Brand Commitments

New identity (binding, from ~/Downloads/01_Logo and ~/Downloads/Linkedin):

- Gradient: linear #898D8C (3%) → #74635A (52%) → #606B6B (100%) — muted sage/brown/slate.
- Cream ink on gradient: #E5E3E0. Black variant ink: #1D1C1B on cream ground.
- Mark: five-element aperture/echo symbol (center barrel + two crescents each side); wordmark "Firstocean" in a neutral grotesk (Helvetica-class).
- Voice: one understated declarative line; lowercase-quiet, no exclamation, no marketing energy.
- Asset sources: SVGs in ~/Downloads/01_Logo/SVG/, favicons in ~/Downloads/01_Logo/Favicon/, gradient in ~/Downloads/01_Logo/Gradient/gradient.svg.

## Evidence on Hand

Backers are real: Entrepreneurs First, Transpose Platform. Booking link (Calendly) exists but is not the chosen contact path. No testimonials, no customers to name — do not fabricate.

## Product Principles

- Restraint is the credibility signal: the less the page says, the more established it reads.
- The identity (mark + gradient) carries the page; copy supports it.
- Nothing invented: no claims, numbers, or clients beyond what exists.
- One action: get in touch.
